Amy Wang

At Lila Sciences, I lead work on evaluations and environments for post-training LLMs for large-molecule biologics. My focus is distilling scientific decision-making into models that can guide closed-loop design cycles, from choosing experiments and interpreting results to coordinating scientific operations.

Previously, I was a Senior ML Scientist at Prescient Design (Genentech), where I led computational strategy for three antibody programs and built ML models for protein design, affinity prediction, and developability optimization. This work led to methods adopted across the antibody portfolio and shaped my interest in automating drug discovery decision-making.

My work draws on both machine learning and experimental biophysics. I earned my PhD in Chemical Engineering from Stanford in 2023, studying force-dependent cell adhesion proteins, and worked on protein–polymer systems for drug delivery as an undergraduate at MIT.
